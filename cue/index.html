get cue

a video and music player for windows

cue.

the video is the interface.

get it on microsoft store download on github
version 1.4 · free · windows x64scroll

01 · the window

just the film. nothing else.

cue opens in the shape of your video. no toolbars, no frames, nothing between you and the picture.

02 · the controls

there when you reach for them.

move the mouse and the bar comes back. keep still, and it rests. try it on the picture.

03 · the light

light that follows the scene.

the window glows with the colours of what you're watching, like a screen in a dark room.

04 · new in 1.4

chapters, right on the timeline.

jump between them with pg up and pg dn, or pick one from the ··· menu.

you came to watch a film, not a player. so cue steps aside, and comes back the moment you need it.

music

songs get a stage too.

the cover, sharp in the middle. the spectrum opens like wings beside it, the bass closest, and a soft light in the cover's colours swells with the music.

around the player

everything you need. nothing in the way.

drag it. let go near a corner.

the mini player.

ctrl+m and the film moves to a small window. it stays where you leave it, and glides into a corner when you let go beside one.

your phone is the remote.

a universal inbox for files

omni.

copy, ctrl+v, done.

a small window for videos, images, audio and files. paste a link, or drop a file, and omni downloads, converts or compresses it. the file decides.

the rule omni lives by

omni should never ask a question it can safely answer itself.
